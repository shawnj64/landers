## Part 3 — Testing Notes

**Rotation plan:** Launch all three in one ad set (Advantage+ placements off initially — feed-only for cleaner data). Kill anything under 0.8% CTR after 1,000 impressions.

**Creative pairing:**
- Variation A → relatable "stuck" visuals (clock, commute, parking lot before shift). Avoid stock-photo polish; UGC-style outperforms here.
- Variation B → text-on-screen hook, plain background, screen-record scroll of the advertorial.
- Variation C → curriculum/screen walkthrough. Show the actual path structure — this angle lives on proof, not emotion.

**Hook swaps:** Hooks 1–10 are written to drop directly into the first line of any variation. Test hooks against the winning body copy, not the other way around — hook is where 80% of the CTR movement is.

**Bridge page must-haves:** The advertorial has to deliver on the specific promise in the ad headline before any link out. Ad says "here's what finally worked" → the page tells that story, *then* transitions. Mismatch here is the #1 killer of these funnels, and Meta will flag it.
